About Us

Rooted in community, built on trust

Founded in 2008, Haven & Hearth Realty began with a simple belief: every family deserves a place to call home. Over the past 15+ years we have grown from a two-person office into a full-service agency serving the greater Maplewood region and beyond.

We combine local market knowledge with a genuine passion for helping people. Our agents take the time to understand your unique needs — whether you are upsizing, downsizing, or relocating across the country.
