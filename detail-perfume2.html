Fleur de Nuit

Floral · Powdery · Eau de Parfum

Fleur de Nuit

La romance en fleurs
Fragrance Family Floral · Powdery
Concentration Eau de Parfum
Launch Year 2023
Perfumer Juliette Moreau

Fleur de Nuit begins with a luminous burst of bergamot, before unveiling its heart of night-blooming jasmine and delicate iris. Ylang-ylang and heliotrope add a creamy, almost powdery softness that is both comforting and sensual.

The base unfolds with velvety musk, warm sandalwood, and a whisper of vanilla, leaving a trail of timeless elegance. This is the scent of a moonlit garden in full bloom — romantic, graceful, utterly captivating.

Fragrance Notes

Bergamot Jasmine Iris Ylang-Ylang Heliotrope Musk Sandalwood Vanilla
