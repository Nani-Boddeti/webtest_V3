Noir Élégance

Oriental · Woody · Eau de Parfum

Noir Élégance

L'élégance des ténèbres
Fragrance Family Oriental · Woody
Concentration Eau de Parfum
Launch Year 2022
Perfumer Catherine Duvall

Noir Élégance opens with a striking burst of blackcurrant and saffron — a dark, fruity spark that gives way to a heart of Rose de Mai and the smoky depth of oud. The base settles into a rich foundation of patchouli, sandalwood, amber, and musk, creating a scent that is both powerful and refined.

This is a fragrance for those who command the room without uttering a word. Perfect for evening soirées and cool autumn days, it lingers on the skin with an irresistible magnetism.

Fragrance Notes

Blackcurrant Saffron Rose de Mai Oud Patchouli Sandalwood Amber Musk
