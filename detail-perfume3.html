Océan Sauvage

Aquatic · Woody · Eau de Toilette

Océan Sauvage

L'appel du large
Fragrance Family Aquatic · Woody
Concentration Eau de Toilette
Launch Year 2024
Perfumer Antoine Lefèvre

Océan Sauvage captures the untamed spirit of the Atlantic. Top notes of sea salt and bergamot evoke the spray of crashing waves, while a heart of seaweed and lavender brings the rugged coastal flora to life.

Juniper berries add a crisp, aromatic freshness that gives way to a base of sun-bleached driftwood, cedar, and ambergris. This is a fragrance for the free-spirited — those who find peace in the roar of the ocean.

Fragrance Notes

Sea Salt Bergamot Seaweed Lavender Juniper Driftwood Cedar Ambergris
