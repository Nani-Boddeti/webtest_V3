Ambre Royal

Amber · Vanilla · Eau de Parfum

Ambre Royal

La chaleur d'un roi
Fragrance Family Amber · Vanilla
Concentration Eau de Parfum
Launch Year 2024
Perfumer Isabelle Fontaine

Ambre Royal wraps you in a warm, golden embrace from the very first spritz. Cinnamon and benzoin open with a spicy sweetness that gives way to a heart of labdanum and tonka bean — rich, resinous, and deeply comforting.

Madagascan vanilla takes centre stage in the base, supported by sandalwood, frankincense, and a whisper of leather. This is a fragrance of quiet majesty — perfect for cold winter nights and intimate gatherings by the fire.

Fragrance Notes

Cinnamon Benzoin Labdanum Tonka Bean Madagascar Vanilla Sandalwood Frankincense Leather
